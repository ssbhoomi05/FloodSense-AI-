Before Floodwater Enters Your Neighborhood

  • Monitor Official Advisories: Keep track of IMD bulletins and Greater Chennai Corporation announcements via radio or battery-powered devices.
  • Clear Terrace Drains: Ensure terrace downpipes and balcony weep-holes are clear of dead leaves to prevent roof ponding.
  • Park Vehicles on Higher Ground: If living in chronic low-lying sectors (Velachery, Madipakkam, Mudichur), relocate vehicles to multi-level parking or elevated flyover margins where designated by traffic police.
  • Disconnect External Drain Valves: Ensure non-return valves are fitted to prevent sewage back-siphonage into ground-floor bathrooms.
  • Charge Everything: Top up inverters, power banks, and electric emergency lanterns while grid power is still stable.

During Active Flooding (Critical Life Safety)

  • Turn Off Main MCB: If floodwater begins entering your ground floor, immediately switch off your home's main electrical breaker (MCB) to prevent fatal electrocution.
  • Turn Around, Don't Drown: Never walk, wade, or drive through flowing water. Just 15 cm (6 inches) of rapid water can knock an adult off their feet; 30 cm (1 foot) can float a car.
  • Open Manhole Hazards: Dislodged manhole covers are the #1 drowning risk on flooded Chennai streets. Never allow children to wade in murky water.
  • Do Not Drink Tap Water: Floodwaters contaminate municipal distribution lines. Boil all drinking water vigorously for at least 1 minute or use chlorine purification tablets.
  • Call GCC 1913 or TNFRS 101: If water level reaches dangerous heights, signal with a whistle from the balcony or terrace and dial 1913 for boat evacuation.

After Floodwaters Recede

  • Watch for Snakes & Scorpions: Reptiles seek refuge in higher dry spots inside houses, cupboards, and rolled mattresses. Inspect corners with a flashlight before reaching in.
  • Do Not Switch Power Back On Blindly: Have a licensed electrician inspect submerged wiring, outlets, and inverters before energizing the main switch.
  • Disinfect Sump & Overhead Tanks: Drain floodwater from sumps, scrub with bleaching powder solution (chlorination), and flush thoroughly before refilling.
  • Prevent Leptospirosis (Rat Fever): Wear waterproof rubber boots during clean-up. Wash cuts immediately with soap and consult a GCC primary health centre for prophylactic doxycycline if advised.
  • Document Property Damage: Take clear photographs and video of water levels and damaged goods before disposal for insurance claims.

Electrical & Water Contamination Protocols

⚡ Electrical Shock Hazards

TANGEDCO de-energizes distribution feeders in waterlogged neighborhoods to prevent mass electrocution.

  • Stay at least 10 metres away from snapped power cables.
  • Never touch metal lampposts or transformers submerged in standing water.
  • Report sparks or fallen lines immediately to TANGEDCO Helpline: 94987 94987.

💧 Safe Water Protocols

Waterborne pathogens (cholera, typhoid, E. coli) surge in standing urban runoff.

  • Boil water at a rolling boil for a full 60 seconds.
  • If fuel is unavailable, use chlorine tablets (halazone) as directed.
  • Discard any food item that touched floodwater, including canned items with dented or rusted seams.
